# Surface: VAHL home (single-page scroll story)

Scope: the whole site, one route. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The visitor decides and acts, and the experience carries the persuasion.

- Audience: collectors of independent watchmaking (see PRODUCT.md).
- Action: request an allocation. This is the only conversion. The form is client-side only and the fiction is disclosed in the footer.
- Proof: the movement itself. It comes apart, the remontoir releases once a second, and the finishing is labelled with real dimensions. No testimonials, press, prices or logos.
- Constraints: one watch, one calibre. Stack pinned (Next.js, TypeScript, R3F, GSAP/ScrollTrigger, Lenis). Reduced-motion and mobile get their own deliberate compositions.
- Memorable moment: the cursor loupe over the disassembling movement, and the screen drenched in heat-blued steel while the remontoir lets go once a second.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site *is* the Kaliber 01's surface under a watchmaker's loupe. It refuses the category's black-and-gold macro showcase, the centred hero and the specs grid.

OWN-WORLD: A frosted German-silver ground with fine grain. Type is engraved and lacquer-filled, set in Archivo (light and expanded for display, expanded caps for engravings). Heat-blued steel is the one saturated colour and fills whole viewports. Ruby marks live state only. Gold appears only on chatons. Black-polish bands catch and flash light. Specimen labels use tabular numerals, scale bars are in mm, and a 60-tick minute track serves as the progress rail.

STORY: The visitor sees the hand step, watches the movement come apart and the remontoir release once a second, and believes the craft from the labelled parts. The ground darkens toward mørketid, and they request before it closes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The watch, dial side, bleeds off the right two-thirds at loupe scale, its blued seconds hand jumping once a second. "This one steps." is engraved at about 14vw lower left, passing behind the case. The VAHL wordmark sits top left, "Request an allocation" top right, with live Tromsø time and days to close. The cursor is a 4× loupe.

FORM: Under the Loupe, position 6 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key 01619411. Signature interaction: a cursor loupe over a scroll-disassembled movement. Motion grammar: the camera moves continuously, while anything that represents time settles in discrete one-second steps. Raises: blued drench (OSPAAAL), power-path line (Googie), specimen labels (kiln), Tromsø sun and countdown (bazaar), one continuous scene (ebru), one-second settle (bench).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

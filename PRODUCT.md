## Users

- **Primary: collectors of independent watchmaking.** They own several mechanical watches and know the vocabulary: calibre, remontoir, anglage, chatons, beat rate. They come from forums, auction catalogues and fair reports. They visit to judge whether the mechanism and the hand-finishing are real and serious. Their job is to decide whether to spend one of their few yearly "asks" on requesting an allocation.
- **Secondary: watch press, curators and the creative-web community.** They judge the craft of the presentation itself.

## Product Purpose

**VAHL** is a three-person workshop that makes one watch, the **Mørketid**, built around one in-house calibre, the **Kaliber 01**. It produces 24 pieces a year. The website exists to make a collector understand, part by part, why this movement is worth a request. It ends in a single action: **request an allocation** for the coming year.

Success: a qualified collector reaches the end understanding the mechanism and submits a request. Nobody should leave confused about what the watch does differently.

## Positioning

Most mechanical watches *sweep*: the seconds hand glides because the escapement beats 6–8 times a second. The Kaliber 01 *steps*. A constant-force remontoir stores a sliver of the mainspring's energy and releases it **exactly once per second**. The seconds hand jumps in single, deliberate, mechanical steps (*seconde morte*, "dead-beat seconds"). Each second is also delivered with the same force whether the mainspring is full or nearly empty.

Brand line: *continuous energy, counted out in exact seconds.*

Workshop origin: Tromsø, Norway (69°39′N), above the Arctic Circle. *Mørketid* is the Norwegian name for the polar night, roughly 27 November to 15 January, when the sun does not rise. The watch is named after it. Requests close on the first day of mørketid, and allocations are decided by hand during it.

## Operating Context

- Founder and master watchmaker: **Marit Vahl**. Workshop founded 2014. Three watchmakers in total.
- Every component except the jewels and the mainspring is made and finished in the workshop.
- Collectors request, they don't buy. A request form (name, email, country, optional note) opens each year and closes on 27 November. Allocations are confirmed by letter in January. Delivery takes about 18 months.
- Price is **not published**; it is shared on allocation. This is common practice for independents, and it means the site makes no price claim.

## Product Principles

1. **Show the part, then name it.** Every claim about the movement is shown in the mechanism before it is stated in words.
2. **One watch, told completely.** Depth over breadth. No catalogue, no ranges, no tangents.
3. **The second is the unit.** The dead-beat second is the product's single differentiator, and the experience keeps returning to it.
4. **Exactness is the warmth.** Precise numbers, correct terms and real tolerances are what persuade this audience.
5. **A request, not a sale.** The close is an invitation to apply, deliberately unhurried.
